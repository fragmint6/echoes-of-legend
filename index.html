Rulebook

Comprehensive Engine Rules & Mechanics Reference

1. Turn Flow & Round Phasing

  • Alternating Turns: Battles are played in alternating single actions. You make one move with one living legend, then your opponent makes one move, back and forth across the board.
  • Starting Initiative: In singleplayer, campaign, and multiplayer matches, the opening player of Round 1 is decided by a fair 50/50 coin flip. The starting side alternates between odd and even rounds.
  • Round 1 (Phase 1): Basic Skills only. Powerful Signature Skills are locked during the opening round to establish frontline positions and build initial tempo.
  • Round 2 Onward (Phase 2): All Signature Skills unlock, allowing full team combos, ultimate abilities, and tactical counters.
  • Round 4 Onward (Late-Game Ramp): Starting in Round 4, all living legends grow stronger each round, gaining an extra +15% Attack power every round (+15% in Round 4, +30% in Round 5, +45% in Round 6, etc.) to ensure matches resolve decisively.
  • Passing Turns: You may pass your action to save Energy or wait for an enemy response. A round ends only when both players pass in a row or when neither side has any legal moves left.

2. Energy Economy & Carry-over

  • Energy Income: Both teams receive energy at the start of each round: 60 Energy in Round 1, 80 Energy in Round 2, and 100 Energy in Round 3 and every round after.
  • Carry-over Cap: Any unspent Energy at the end of a round carries over and adds to your next round's income, up to a maximum bank of 150 Energy.
  • Comeback Bonus: If you begin a round with fewer surviving legends than your opponent, your team rallies with an extra +15 Energy for each lost hero deficit to help you turn the tide.
  • Skill Costs: Basic skills typically cost 15 Energy (or are free on certain roles), while signature active skills cost between 20 and 55 Energy.

3. Formations & Targeting Laws

  • 6v6 Board Layout: Each squad fields 6 legends arranged into 3 Front Row positions (slots 0, 1, 2) and 3 Back Row positions (slots 3, 4, 5).
  • Melee Frontline Lock: Tanks and Bruisers are close-combat fighters and must attack enemy Front Row legends. They can only strike at the Back Row once every front-line enemy has fallen.
  • Ranged Freedom: Casters, Controllers, Medics, and Snipers can aim their skills at any enemy on the board regardless of row.
  • Broken Frontline Penalty: When an entire Front Row is wiped out, surviving Back Row legends suffer a -5% Defense penalty because their protective vanguard has collapsed.

4. Damage Calculation & Mitigation

  • Damage Power: The damage you deal begins with your legend's Attack stat multiplied by the Skill's power percentage, plus any active attack modifiers and buffs.
  • Defense & Mitigation: That damage is reduced by the enemy's Defense percentage (which reduces incoming damage up to a maximum of 75%) and any active damage resistance barriers (up to 90%).
  • Defense Ceiling: Defense percentage is hard-capped at 75% maximum reduction so that armor never completely reduces damage to zero.
  • Resistance Ceiling: Timed damage resistance barriers (such as Warded) are hard-capped at 90% maximum reduction.
  • Critical Strikes: Critical hits multiply damage by exactly 1.5x (50% bonus damage), determined by your legend's Critical Chance stat.
  • Guaranteed Impact: Every attack that connects is guaranteed to deal at least 1 point of damage.

5. Protection Mechanics

  • Provoke (Taunt): Forces all single-target enemy attacks onto the provoker. Area-of-effect spells and sniper skills that shoot around the provoker deal 30% less damage to all other targets. The provoker always takes full 100% damage.
  • Untargetable: Absolute protection. An Untargetable legend cannot be targeted by any enemy single-target skill under any circumstances.
  • Shields: Absorbs incoming damage point-for-point before your health takes a hit. Burn damage bypasses shields. Overhealing from certain skills (like Medic Restore) converts excess healing into shields.

6. Status Effects & Afflictions

  • Burn: Deals 5% of maximum health as true damage at the start of each turn that team takes. Bypasses Defense and Shields completely.
  • Exposed: Strips away the target's Defense, reducing it to 0% for heavy damage.
  • Marked: Places a target reticle on an enemy. The next damaging Skill consumes the Mark and triggers powerful bonus effects (such as bonus damage or burning). Basic attacks do not consume Marks.
  • Silence: Completely stops a legend from acting — both Signature Skills and Basic attacks are blocked.
  • Warded: Grants flat damage resistance that protects the legend even if they are Exposed.
  • Counter Ready: Automatically strikes back against any attacker when hit while shielded.
  • Healing Reduced: Lowers the amount of healing received from skills. Shields are unaffected.
  • Cost Modifiers: Increases or decreases Energy costs across the entire team (+15% or -15%).

7. Debuff Scaling & Payoffs

  • Debuff Synergies: Distinct debuffs include stat reductions (reduced Attack, Defense, or Crit), Silence, healing cuts, Burn, Exposed, Marked, and increased Energy costs.
  • Debuff Damage Scaling: Legends like Inari (+30% Attack per debuff), Cicero (+15% Attack per debuff), Friar Tuck (+14% Attack per debuff), and Pied Piper (+13% Attack per debuff) deal dramatically higher damage against debuffed enemies.
  • Blessing Stripping: Certain skills strip all positive blessings and shields from enemies to turn the momentum in your favor.
  • Stack Caps: Passive stacking traits enforce battle-wide lifetime stack limits (typically 4 to 5 stacks) to keep power balanced across long fights.

8. The 10 Battlefields & Terrain Laws

  • The Colosseum: Neutral arena where pure tactical skill decides the victor. No special terrain modifiers.
  • The Mirror Realm: The first Signature Skill cast by each player each round repeats a second time for free at 50% power. (Basic attacks do not echo).
  • The Spirit World: The first lethal blow against each legend leaves them alive on 1 HP instead of defeating them (once per legend per battle). A second hit finishes the job.
  • The Narrow Pass: Melee chokepoint — only Front Row legends may use Basic attacks. Back Row legends must spend Energy on real skills to contribute.
  • The Open Plains: Wide open ground — the -5% Backline Defense Penalty when the front row falls is waived.
  • Mana Spring: +20 bonus Energy granted to both teams every round.
  • Energy Void: -10 Energy reduction for both teams every round.
  • The Ancient Ruins: A random ancient relic blessing is granted to all surviving legends at the start of each round.
  • The Hero's Trial: All legends grow in strength every round, gaining +15% Attack at each round rollover.
  • The Blood Battlefield: Defeating any enemy legend immediately rewards your team with +20 bonus Energy.

9. Draft & Deck Construction

  • Deck Composition: A legal deck contains exactly 12 legends with a strict cap of at most 4 legends of any single role (Tanks, Bruisers, Casters, Controllers, Medics, Snipers).
  • Snake Draft: 12 packs of 3 legends. Players take turns opening packs and picking 1 card each; the 3rd card in each pack burns away until both squads reach 12 legends.
  • Unabridged Match Sets: A competitive best-of-3 series. Between games, the losing player selects the next battlefield and players may swap 1 to 2 legends from their reserve bench.
