Rulebook

The rules in plain words - with pictures and examples

New here? Gate I of the campaign teaches all of this by hand - play it first. This book is the quick reference for when you just need one rule or one number. And the little ? dots scattered around every screen explain whatever they sit beside - hover them.

The Game in 30 Seconds

  1. Bring 12 legends. Build a deck, or draft one on the spot.
  2. Ban 2 of theirs. They ban 2 of yours. Both sealed, revealed together - no peeking.
  3. Field 6 of your 10. Three up front, three in back.
  4. Fight. One action at a time, until one team falls.

A ban is a question: what can this player not live without? Crown cards are protected, so strike the support or combo piece that lets their legend take over.

How a Round Works

  1. A coin flip decides who opens round 1. Odd and even rounds alternate the opener.
  2. On your turn: pick one legend, use one skill - or pass.
  3. Your opponent does the same. Back and forth.
  4. When both sides pass in a row, the round ends and fresh energy arrives.
R1 basics only R2+ signatures unlock R4+ everyone gains +15% ATK per round

Passing is not surrender. Pass early, bank the energy, and drop a huge signature next round while your rival is broke.

Energy: the Only Currency

Round 1 60
Round 2 80
Round 3+ 100
  • Unspent energy carries over - your bank holds up to 150.
  • Basic skills cost about 15. Signatures run 20 to 55.
  • Losing? You rally. Start a round with fewer legends alive than the enemy and you get +15 energy per legend you are down.

Round 2, with 35 saved: 80 + 35 = 115 energy. That is a 55-cost signature and four basics in one round.

The Board: Rows & Roles

Back works in peace
Front takes the hits

The melee law: Tanks and Bruisers must chew through the enemy front row before they can touch the back. Everyone else aims anywhere.

Tank - soaks hits, provokes enemies into hitting him.
Bruiser - front-row muscle; hits hard, heals off the wounds.
Medic - undoes damage. Guard yours; kill theirs first.
Caster - glass cannon; magic in wide, cruel shapes.
Sniper - reaches the back row where soft targets hide.
Controller - bends the fight: weakens, exposes, redirects.

If your whole front row falls, the survivors fight scared: -5% defense until the end.

How Damage Works

  1. Start big: your Attack × the skill's power %.
  2. Armor shaves it: the target's defense % comes off (capped at 75%).
  3. Crits multiply: a critical hit deals exactly 1.5×.
Worked example: a Sniper with 1,000 ATK casts a 180% signature → 1,800 raw. The target has 30% defense → 1,260 damage. If it crits → 1,890.

Timed damage barriers (Warded) cap at 90%. No hit ever deals less than 1 damage. Armor never makes anyone immortal.

Keeping People Alive

Provoke - "hit ME." Single-target attacks must obey. Splash damage around the provoker is 30% weaker - he always takes the full 100%.
Untargetable - cannot be picked by any single-target skill. Absolute, no exceptions.
Shields - absorb damage point-for-point before health. Burn sneaks past them. Some overhealing (Medic Restore) turns into shield.

Classic opening: Tank provokes, enemy wastes a round punching the wall, your Caster works in peace behind it.

Status Effects at a Glance

Burn - 5% of max HP every turn. Ignores armor and shields.
Exposed - defense stripped to 0%. Hit them NOW.
Marked - the next damaging skill detonates it for a bonus. Basics do not.
Silence - no skills, no basics, no acting at all.
Warded - flat damage resistance. Works even while Exposed.
Counter - strikes back automatically when hit while shielded.
Healing cut - heals do less. Shields are unaffected.
Cost shift - the whole team's skills cost +15% or -15%.

Debuffs are ammunition: legends like Inari, Cicero and the Pied Piper hit harder for every debuff on the target. Stack miseries, then swing. (Lifetime stack traits cap at 4-5 per battle.)

Decks, Drafts & Sets

  • A legal deck: exactly 12 legends, never more than 4 of one role.
  • Classic: bring a saved deck. Draft: build from nothing - packs of 3, you pick one, the rival picks one, the last card burns.
  • The Set (best of 3): after each game the loser picks the next arena, and both sides may swap 1-2 legends in from the bench.

Lost game one to a burn deck? Pick the arena that suits YOU, bench the slow pieces, bring the Medic. The sideboard is where sets are won.

The Ten Arenas

Every arena bends the rules its own way - read its card before each war. Mid-fight, the badge in the board's top-left corner names the ground; hover it whenever you forget.
