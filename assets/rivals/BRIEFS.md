# Chapter 1 Rival Card-Art Briefs

These are original card-art identities for the Chapter 1 rivals. They follow the
normal Echoes of Legend portrait rules: one three-quarter-length figure, a clear
face in the upper third, a dark full-scene backdrop, an upper-left cool key with
a warm element rim, selective outlines, clustered 16-bit shading, and no text or
secondary figures.

| Rival | Card direction |
| --- | --- |
| **The Recruiter** `rare / Controller / Nature` | A lean old memory broker with a deeply lined dark face, salt-and-pepper locs, a close gray beard and a bronze memory monocle. His patched moss-green road coat, massive satchel and sealed glass memories make him a warm, exacting guide through Grimmwood mist rather than a fairy-tale wizard. |
| **The Oathkeeper** `epic / Tank / Light` | An aged male war veteran with an iron-gray stubble beard, clouded eye, torn ear and battle-scarred nose. His battered open-face helm, repaired blue-and-off-white plate, frayed oath sash and dented kite shield make him a patient Camelot survivor, never a crowned king. |
| **The Outlaw** `rare / Sniper / Physical` | A freckled, copper-haired young sniper in a mustard riding coat and ink-blue scarf. Her long antique blackpowder sniper rifle with brass tube sight replaces the familiar longbow entirely; the ruined forest watch platform makes her a different kind of Sherwood threat. |
| **The Anointed** `legendary / Caster / Light` | A tall, bronze-skinned elder priestess with a shaved head, an asymmetric gold sun-disc crown and temple-blue geometric face paint. Her white prayer wraps and bronze astrolabe create luminous marks below her hands in a dark marble Olympus sanctuary. |
| **The Warden of the Mid-Road** `legendary / Tank / Shadow` | A broad-shouldered East Asian road adjudicator with a burned shaved temple, a silver hoop and stern dark eyes. Asymmetrical black-slate plate over an ivory surveyor’s wrap, a burgundy mantle, iron gate-key and measuring cord make her the living rule of the three toll arches—not another anonymous sentinel. |
| **The Trickster** `epic / Controller / Magic` | A young adult woman with short black hair tipped plum, bright clever eyes, gold hoops and a vermilion gambler’s jacket lined in teal. Her laughing fan of blank lacquer tiles and loose, kinetic stance make her a playful Yamato hustler with no mask or courtly disguise. |
| **The Strategist** `epic / Controller / Magic` | An old man with a bald crown, long silver side-hair, a narrow gray beard and an architect’s bronze lens. His charcoal scholar’s robe and Tyrian-purple planner’s mantle carry measuring pins rather than military laurels; he studies violet geometry over a blank wax board. |
| **The Chronicler** `epic / Controller / Shadow` | A gaunt, ash-pale archivist with shaved sides, short white hair and ink-dark fingertips. Charcoal-and-shrine-white robes, a broken vermilion spectacle chain and blank drifting folios make this a weary keeper of names, not a moon princess. |
| **The Last Guardian** `legendary / Tank / Lightning` | A tall dark-skinned sentinel in a weathered squared ivory-and-charcoal gate helmet. Its raised visor and lightning-blue crack frame, rather than hide, her determined eyes; jade gate plate and the enormous wooden key on her back make her final-road vigil unmistakable. |
| **Gilgamesh** `legendary / Bruiser-Caster / Light` | The First Legend is treated as the hero-king of walled Uruk rather than a medieval monarch: thick curled hair, bead-weighted beard, simple woven royal headband, fringed wool robe, lapis bead belt and bronze arm rings. His background is mud-brick Uruk beneath a star band; no living lion or anachronistic crown intrudes on the epic silhouette. |
