# Chapter 1 Rival Card-Art Briefs

These are original card-art identities for the Chapter 1 rivals. They follow the
normal Echoes of Legend portrait rules: one three-quarter-length figure, a clear
face in the upper third, a dark full-scene backdrop, an upper-left cool key with
a warm element rim, selective outlines, clustered 16-bit shading, and no text or
secondary figures.

| Rival | Card direction |
| --- | --- |
| **The Recruiter** `rare / Controller / Nature` | An ageless memory broker with a weathered brown face, silver micro-braids and a high moss-green wayfarer collar. Her violet-stitch travel coat is hung with sealed glass memories; her open hand coaxes a single pale green echo-light through Grimmwood mist. She is warm, precise and not a fairy-tale royal. |
| **The Oathkeeper** `epic / Tank / Light` | A scarred, middle-aged knightly woman with a cropped white-blonde undercut and a dark iron cheek guard, not a crowned king. Blue enamel bell-plate, a white oath sash and a low kite shield make a patient, immovable Camelot silhouette. |
| **The Outlaw** `rare / Sniper / Physical` | A wiry, copper-haired rooftop raider in a mustard riding coat, ink-blue scarf and mismatched leather bracers. They use a compact crank crossbow rather than a longbow; the sharp, amused face and urban forest lookout keep them distinct from Sherwood’s Robin Hood. |
| **The Anointed** `legendary / Caster / Light` | A tall, bronze-skinned elder priestess with a shaved head, an asymmetric gold sun-disc crown and temple-blue geometric face paint. Her white prayer wraps and bronze astrolabe create luminous marks below her hands in a dark marble Olympus sanctuary. |
| **The Warden of the Mid-Road** `legendary / Tank / Shadow` | An elderly road sentinel with a copper eye patch, cropped white hair and a long cloak pieced from faded gate-tolls. Black-stone pauldrons, an iron chain lantern and a low spear identify a guardian who has watched too many crossings, not another Camelot knight. |
| **The Trickster** `epic / Controller / Magic` | A young stage-game hustler with a shaved temple, bright red coat, teal lining and a stack of lacquered game tiles at one wrist. No fox mask or court robes: their sly grin and low-slung fan of blank tiles are a playful Yamato counterpoint. |
| **The Strategist** `epic / Controller / Magic` | An older Black woman with close-cropped salt hair, a gold architect’s lens over one eye and a deep Tyrian-purple planner’s mantle. She wears no laurel and no cuirass; violet geometry rises from a blank wax board in the shadowed Roman forum. |
| **The Chronicler** `epic / Controller / Shadow` | A gaunt, ash-pale archivist with shaved sides, short white hair and ink-dark fingertips. Charcoal-and-shrine-white robes, a broken vermilion spectacle chain and blank drifting folios make this a weary keeper of names, not a moon princess. |
| **The Last Guardian** `legendary / Tank / Lightning` | A tall sentinel woman with a shaved head, dark skin, diamond-shaped silver temple marks and layered ivory, jade and charcoal gate armour. A giant wooden key is worn across her back; she stands before the final road with a steadiness that needs no crown. |
| **Gilgamesh** `legendary / Bruiser-Caster / Light` | The First Legend is a broad Bronze Age king with dark hair braided into three heavy cords, a bead-weighted beard and a stepped copper crown. A lion-hide mantle, lapis cylinder seals and worn bronze scale skirt make an unmistakably Uruk silhouette as judgement light gathers at his hand. |
