# Chapter 1 Rival Card-Art Briefs

These are the canonical visual briefs for Chapter 1 after the narrative pass in
`docs/LORE-Campaign-Chapter1.md`. Every image is a serious, full-art card
illustration: one three-quarter-length figure, a readable face at roughly the
upper quarter, a dark complete environment, and only a **narrow upper-left
1–2px element rim**. Never use a glow or outline around the entire figure.

| Rival | Card direction |
| --- | --- |
| **The Recruiter** `rare / Controller / Nature` | An old male memory broker at the beginning of the Road. Lean, deeply lined, dark-skinned; short gray beard; salt-and-pepper locs tied back; bronze monocle. Moss-green patched road coat, oversized ledger satchel, vials of collected memories. His authority is gentle, weary, and administrative—not wizardly or comic. |
| **The Oathkeeper** `epic / Tank / Light` | An aged male war veteran who promised to bring forty-three people home and returned with twelve. Weather-browned face, clouded left eye, torn ear, old scars, iron-gray stubble. Battered open-faced helm, repaired blue-and-off-white plate, frayed oath sash, deeply dented kite shield. He is a survivor carrying the cost of a promise, not a king. |
| **The Outlaw** `rare / Sniper / Physical` | A copper-haired young woman from a bookshop family whose records were burned before its books. Mustard riding coat, ink-blue scarf, leather gloves and an antique long blackpowder sniper rifle with brass tube sight. Practical, amused, unsentimental; no green hood, no bow, no Robin Hood shape. |
| **The Anointed** `legendary / Caster / Light` | An elder priestess who learned that a warning can be a blade held politely. Bronze skin, shaved head, asymmetric beaten-gold sun disc, temple-blue face paint, white prayer wraps, indigo mantle and bronze astrolabe. Her marks sit below the face as quiet geometric promise-lights. |
| **The Warden of the Mid-Road** `legendary / Tank / Shadow` | A female road adjudicator who stays where the Road’s three exams meet. Burn scar on a shaved temple, silver hoop, black-slate plate over an ivory surveyor’s wrap, burgundy mantle, huge iron gate-key and measuring cord. A low staff carries an unlit signal lantern. She reads as the living law of the toll arches, not a generic armored sentinel. |
| **The Trickster** `epic / Controller / Magic` | A young adult woman who was written as background in her first story and learned to deal herself into other ones. Bright, alert eyes; short black hair tipped plum; gold hoops; vermilion gambler jacket with teal lining; lacquered blank tiles at her wrist. Her energy is sharp and mischievous, never cute or childish. |
| **The Strategist** `epic / Controller / Magic` | An old male planner who saved a city by choosing which villages would burn. Bald crown, long silver side-hair, narrow gray beard, bronze architect’s lens, charcoal scholar robe and Tyrian-purple mantle with measuring pins. He studies violet lines above a blank wax board; no laurel, cuirass, or emperor silhouette. |
| **The Chronicler** `epic / Controller / Shadow` | A gaunt ash-pale archivist in the wall-less Spirit World library. Shaved sides, short white hair, ink-dark fingers and broken vermilion spectacle chain; charcoal and shrine-white robes; blank pages and violet ink lower in frame. They should feel severe and exhausted, not ethereal or princess-like. |
| **The Last Guardian** `legendary / Tank / Lightning` | A dark-skinned woman who gave up her name to keep the Quiet from crossing the final gate. Her squared ivory-and-charcoal gate helmet has a raised narrow visor and a lightning-blue brow crack that frames clear, tired eyes. Jade, ivory and charcoal historical-fantasy gate plate, with a giant wooden key across her back. |
| **Gilgamesh** `legendary / Bruiser-Caster / Light` | The legend-king of walled Uruk: broad, grave, strong, with thick curled dark hair, a bead-weighted curled beard, woven royal headband, fringed wool robe/skirt, lapis bead belt and bronze arm rings. He carries an old bronze sword low. Mud-brick Uruk and temple towers stand under a cold star band. Avoid medieval crowns, generic paladin armor, and living lion companions. |
